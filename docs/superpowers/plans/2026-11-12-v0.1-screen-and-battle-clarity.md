# Latentia v0.1: screen, battle clarity, art pass 2

> **For agentic workers:** this plan uses superpowers:subagent-driven-development with a lighter cadence. Each task gets one implementer. Q1 and Q3 run in parallel, in separate worktrees. There is one independent review after Q4, then Travis playtests.

**Goal:** fix the two things Travis's v0 playtest found.
1. The game image is small on a phone.
2. Battles are confusing, even though they are well explained.

Do it without changing the engine's battle rules.

**Source:** Travis's playtest feedback on 2026-11-11 ("Screen is fairly small… increase that and the details of the graphics", "battling is a bit silly as it's kind of confusing. But it's explained well"). On 2026-11-12 he approved this battle direction:
- **Normal battles** become simpler turns with telegraphs, on the same engine. This is the work in this plan.
- **Gyms** become eval puzzles: fixed foe behaviour; you win by teaching the card that beats it. Later.
- **Ghost battles** use coach mode: set your cards before the fight, plus 2–3 mid-fight prompts. Later.

**Base:** branch `game/v0.1`, cut from `game/playable-v0` at cf1ffe4 (worktree /home/claude/latentia-game). The art work happens on `art/pass-2`, cut from `origin/main` (worktree /home/claude/latentia-art).

## Global Constraints
- **The engine is not changed in behaviour.**
  - A missing **public export** may be added to `@latentia/engine`, but only as a read-only helper. It needs tests and a patch version bump (0.4.2), and the golden hashes must not change.
  - The client still mutates engine state only through `submitRound`.
- **Logical resolution:**
  - Landscape and desktop: **480×270**.
  - Portrait: **270×480**. The short side is fixed at 270. The long side may grow from 480 up to 540 to fill the screen at the chosen integer device-pixel scale.
  - Scenes lay out from the current viewport size, never from constants.
  - Rotating re-lays out the current scene and keeps its state.
- **Scale:** the integer **device-pixel** scale that fills the most screen. Use nearest filtering. Nothing may be scaled by a fractional amount on the device-pixel grid.
- **Tile and sprite sizes:**
  - overworld tiles 24×24 (previously 16);
  - overworld characters 24×32 frames;
  - battle creatures 96×96 frames (previously 64).
- **Palette:** Endesga 32 only (the list is in packages/art/src/build.mjs `PALETTE`).
- **Phone menus live inside the game image, with no separate DOM band.**
  - The touch D-pad and A/B are a translucent DOM overlay at the screen edges, with tap targets ≥ 44 CSS px.
  - Every in-game menu option is also tappable directly, with a hit area ≥ 44 CSS px.
- **Unchanged from v0:**
  - copy rules (no trademark echoes; the copy-guard test stays);
  - saves (bump the schema only if the data changes, and migrate);
  - no external network;
  - the dev hook stays out of production;
  - `build:single` still produces one self-contained file;
  - files stay under about 300 lines;
  - root `pnpm lint && pnpm typecheck && pnpm test` and the game e2e pass;
  - commits are authored as CosmonautJones with no Co-Authored-By line, made after every working step.

## Rulings made while planning
- **V11, foe intents:**
  - The foe's **intent is always telegraphed by category**, Slay the Spire style. One of: Attack (with an estimated damage number, e.g. "~6"), Guard, Buff, Debuff, Special. Plus its target.
  - Reveal effects (Red Team, Saliency Map, Socratic Method) now upgrade the telegraph to the **exact move**, instead of revealing a "?". So reveal moves keep their value.
  - This supersedes V2's "?" for the main battles.
  - Why: an unreadable foe was the biggest source of confusion.
  - Cost if wrong: less mystery, and the "?" can come back for specific bosses.
- **V12, temperature:**
  - The **temperature dial is hidden and fixed at each side's configured temperature** until a save flag `unlocks.temperature` is set. Nothing sets it in v0.1; it is meant for the first gym.
  - Cost if wrong: one fewer lever early on.
- **V13, context:** context shows as **pips** (1 pip per context point, capacity shown as empty pips). Each move shows its cost in pips.
- **V14, disclosure:** each fight shows only what has been taught so far.
  - **Kip:** HP, moves, context pips, foe telegraph.
  - **Wild Tokenibble:** the same.
  - **Bootcamp Grad:** adds the teammate's telegraph, written as "Tokenibble will: Early Exit → Vibrat (its card: IF foe HP < 50%)".
  - Move details are plain words, e.g. "Hits hard. Costs 3. Never misses.", not "PWR 70 ACC 100".
  - Numbers stay available behind a details toggle (the LOG / WHY? area).
- **V15, battle sprite facing:** each battle creature is drawn once, facing the viewer's left (its enemy-side pose). The player's side mirrors it horizontally. Back sprites come later.

## Review Focus
1. Rotating mid-battle, mid-dialogue, mid-menu and mid-two-tap: the state is kept, nothing is cut off, and input still works.
2. A 360×640 Android portrait screen and a 390×844 iPhone: the chosen scale and logical size fill the screen. The game image covers ≥ 70% of the screen area in landscape and ≥ 55% in portrait.
3. The foe telegraph is honest. The "~N" estimate must stay within the engine's actual damage roll range for that move and target in a probe of 300 random rounds (the test asserts this). "Attack" is never shown for a move that deals no damage.
4. Old saves (v3) load into the v0.1 build at every step.
5. `build:single` still plays inside `<iframe sandbox="allow-scripts">`.
